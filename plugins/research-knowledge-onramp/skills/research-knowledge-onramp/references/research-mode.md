# Research Mode

Use this mode when the user wants to understand a new field, a research area, or a method family.

## Default output order

1. Problem landscape
2. Core concept map
3. Terminology and notation
4. Classic methods to current methods
5. Datasets, tasks, and metrics
6. Open questions and gaps
7. Next papers or next steps

## For each concept or method

- What problem does it solve?
- Why is this method needed?
- What assumptions does it make?
- Where does it fail?
- How do researchers evaluate it?
- What is the most likely follow-up question?

## Three-level explanation

- 本科层: intuition and vocabulary
- 研究生层: mechanism, assumptions, and comparison
- 博士层: boundary, novelty, critique, and extension

## Good research outputs

- knowledge map
- paper reading order
- method comparison
- gap analysis
- project sketch
