# Usage Playbook

Use these prompt shapes to activate the skill well.

## Learn a new field

`Use $research-knowledge-onramp to build a research-first onramp for this topic from my local materials. Explain each core concept at undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral levels.`

## Read a paper with mentor-style guidance

`Use $research-knowledge-onramp to read this paper like a paper mentor. Show the problem, real novelty, evidence strength, hidden assumptions, missing baselines, and the best next papers to read.`

## Turn an idea into a proposal

`Use $research-knowledge-onramp as a proposal assistant. Turn this idea into a gap statement, research question, hypothesis, baseline plan, metrics, risks, and a minimum viable experiment.`

## Convert research understanding into exam notes

`Use $research-knowledge-onramp to compress this research-first material into a chapter review, formula notes, and a fast memorization sheet without losing the core logic.`
