# Paper Reading and Deconstruction

Use this when the user gives a paper, an abstract, or a reading list.

## Reading order

1. Research problem
2. Main idea
3. Data and experimental setup
4. Method details
5. Results
6. Claims and evidence
7. Limitations
8. What to try next

## Reading card template

- Title
- Problem
- Key contribution
- Method
- Data
- Metrics
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Reproducibility notes
- One follow-up idea

## Deconstruction rules

- Separate paper claims from your inference
- Separate the method from its implementation details
- Note if the result depends on a narrow dataset or setting
- Point out what would need to be tested to trust the claim
