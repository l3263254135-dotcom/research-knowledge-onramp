# Research Design and Gap Finding

Use this when the user wants a topic idea, a proposal sketch, or a direction to explore.

## Start from a gap

- What is not solved yet?
- What breaks in the current method?
- What is expensive, unstable, or hard to scale?
- What is unclear in the literature?

## Candidate question checklist

- Is the question specific?
- Can it be tested?
- Can it be compared against a baseline?
- Is the dataset or evidence realistic?
- Does it have a clear evaluation metric?

## Proposal sketch

- Research question
- Motivation
- Related work map
- Hypothesis
- Method or technical route
- Baseline comparison
- Evaluation metrics
- Risks and limitations
- Expected contribution
